NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the package prices fixed?

No. Prices on this page are sample starting estimates. Actual prices depend on travel dates, availability, flights, hotel choices and package inclusions.

Are international flights included?

Flights are included only when specifically stated in the final package quotation. Confirm this before booking.

Will I need a visa for my destination?

Entry requirements depend on your passport, destination and travel dates. Check current official requirements before making travel arrangements.

Can I customise my package?

You can contact Travelora to discuss preferred travel dates, hotel options, sightseeing interests and your budget. Customisation is subject to availability and confirmation.

How do I enquire about a package?

Select Enquire Now on your preferred destination card or visit the Contact page to share your travel requirements.
