CITY & DESERT
UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

Dubai Getaway

Experience a striking mix of modern architecture, lively shopping districts, desert landscapes and memorable city views.

Suggested duration 4–5 days Best for Families, couples, friends

Highlights

  • Burj Khalifa and Downtown Dubai
  • Desert safari experience
  • Dubai Marina and waterfront views
  • Shopping and local dining

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive, settle in and explore the nearby area.
  2. Day 2: Visit Downtown Dubai and enjoy city sightseeing.
  3. Day 3: Experience a desert safari and sunset views.
  4. Day 4: Explore the marina, markets or shopping districts.
  5. Day 5: Enjoy free time before departure.

TROPICAL ESCAPE
INDONESIA

Bali Escape

Slow down among tropical beaches, lush rice terraces, beautiful temples and relaxing island scenery.

Suggested duration 5–6 days Best for Couples, friends, nature lovers

Highlights

  • Ubud and green rice terraces
  • Traditional temples and local culture
  • Beach sunsets and coastal cafés
  • Optional wellness and adventure activities

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive and enjoy a relaxed evening.
  2. Day 2: Explore Ubud, its markets and nearby landscapes.
  3. Day 3: Visit temples and scenic countryside.
  4. Day 4: Enjoy beach time or optional water activities.
  5. Day 5: Choose a relaxed day for cafés, shopping or wellness.
  6. Day 6: Depart after breakfast.

THAILAND

Thailand Adventure

Combine vibrant city life, cultural landmarks, lively markets and the tropical beauty of Thailand's coast and islands.

Suggested duration 5–7 days Best for Friends, couples, explorers

Highlights

  • Temples and cultural landmarks
  • Street food and colourful markets
  • Optional island and boat excursions
  • Coastal scenery and local experiences

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive and explore the local area.
  2. Day 2: Visit cultural landmarks and temples.
  3. Day 3: Discover local markets and Thai food.
  4. Day 4: Enjoy a coastal or island excursion.
  5. Day 5: Relax or explore more of the destination.
  6. Days 6–7: Add activities or enjoy a relaxed departure day.

CITY DISCOVERY
SINGAPORE

Singapore Discovery

Explore a modern city known for its waterfront skyline, green spaces, cultural neighbourhoods and diverse food scene.

Suggested duration 3–5 days Best for Families, couples, city explorers

Highlights

  • Marina Bay waterfront
  • Gardens and green spaces
  • Neighbourhoods and cultural districts
  • Local food and shopping

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive and take an evening waterfront walk.
  2. Day 2: Explore Marina Bay and nearby attractions.
  3. Day 3: Visit gardens and cultural neighbourhoods.
  4. Day 4: Enjoy optional family attractions or shopping.
  5. Day 5: Have a relaxed morning before departure.

ISLAND RETREAT
MALDIVES

Maldives Retreat

Unwind beside clear turquoise waters, discover colourful marine life and enjoy the calm of an island holiday.

Suggested duration 3–5 days Best for Couples, honeymooners, relaxation

Highlights

  • White-sand beaches and lagoon views
  • Optional snorkelling and water activities
  • Sunset moments by the sea
  • Leisure time at your chosen resort or island

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive and transfer to your selected island stay.
  2. Day 2: Enjoy the beach and explore the island.
  3. Day 3: Choose optional snorkelling or a boat excursion.
  4. Day 4: Relax and enjoy the island scenery.
  5. Day 5: Depart according to your transfer schedule.

ALPINE ADVENTURE
SWITZERLAND

Switzerland Escape

Discover dramatic alpine scenery, peaceful lakes, charming villages and memorable mountain journeys.

Suggested duration 5–7 days Best for Nature lovers, couples, families

Highlights

  • Swiss alpine villages and viewpoints
  • Scenic lakes and mountain landscapes
  • Optional mountain railways and cable cars
  • Local cuisine and relaxed village walks

Suggested itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrive and settle into your chosen city or village.
  2. Day 2: Explore the town and nearby lakeside scenery.
  3. Day 3: Take a scenic mountain excursion.
  4. Day 4: Visit a traditional village or enjoy a scenic rail journey.
  5. Day 5: Enjoy free time for walks and local experiences.
  6. Days 6–7: Add another region if desired, then depart.
